# Deep research

Read this alongside [Default research](default-research.md) for an important,
costly, unfamiliar, or unresolved decision. Search broadly enough to compare
the plausible solution classes and support the recommendation.

Cover the applicable lanes from the entry skill, following useful terminology,
citations, implementations, alternatives, and related projects. Verify the
leading candidates and any significant rejected alternatives with primary
sources. For mathematics, use [Mathematical research](mathematical-research.md)
to select relevant theorem, formalization, and literature sources.

Keep notes on sources, candidates, and consequential gaps as useful for the
task or handoff. For service searches, retain the helper's coverage and
failure information; a bounded or incomplete search may leave a material
gap. When independent subagents are available and authorized, they can
research separate lanes; verify and synthesize their findings.

Stop when the evidence supports the decision and further searching is
unlikely to change it. If a missing source or unresolved question could change
the recommendation, explain the gap and the next useful check. A thorough
search is still bounded evidence, not proof that no other solution exists.

Use the [Reuse Shortlist response format](reuse-shortlist.md). Summarize the
coverage and findings relevant to the recommendation; include detailed notes
only when requested or needed to understand the result.
