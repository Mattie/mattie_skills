# Journal and comparable formal submission

Activate this mode only when the user explicitly wants to prepare or send a paper to a journal, refereed proceedings, or a comparable formal venue. An ordinary Lean proof review, deep audit, manuscript draft, preprint, or GitHub release does not activate it. Outside this mode, any advice here is optional and should remain a light recommendation when relevant.

Use the [prose and contribution guidance](publication.md) and [artifact evidence guidance](artifact-validation.md) for the underlying review. This mode adds the requirements of the user's chosen submission route; it does not create a universal journal checklist or authorize submission, author contact, or arranging paid or external review.

## Establish the submission requirements

For the selected venue and submission type, consult current official author instructions. Distinguish binding requirements from mathematical-community advice and our own recommendations. Identify the actual requirements relevant to the work, such as author responsibility, originality and attribution, AI-assistance disclosure, formal artifacts, or supplementary evidence. Record what satisfies each applicable requirement and any remaining gap.

If no venue has been chosen, prepare general submission advice and identify which venue-dependent requirements remain unknown. Do not invent a policy or make every possible requirement a gate. Before describing the work as ready for a particular submission, resolve its applicable requirements or state the remaining limits explicitly.

## Author understanding and specialist review

For a substantive mathematical contribution, recommend appropriate human mathematical review more strongly in this mode. It can help assess the central argument, manuscript correspondence, and claimed contribution. Keep the recommendation specific to the work and the user's submission goal.

Treat specialist review as required when the user explicitly requests it or the chosen venue's applicable policy requires it. Formal-submission intent alone does not establish that a journal requires an external specialist review before submission. If the user or policy specifies author understanding or review obligations, establish the evidence needed for those obligations before claiming they are satisfied.

Report the documented scope of author and specialist review, including unresolved steps and any unknown status. Several model reviews can provide scrutiny but do not establish independent human mathematical understanding, peer review, or venue acceptance. Missing records do not establish that understanding is absent. When required review is outstanding, identify the particular unmet requirement; its absence alone does not invalidate a checked mathematical result.

## Describe submission readiness accurately

Reconcile the manuscript's claims, contribution and attribution, exact formal declarations, artifact evidence, and the applicable venue requirements. Check that any claimed reviewer access matches the intended submission audience. Use the [GitHub guidance](github-submission.md) as well if that is the artifact distribution route.

Distinguish a checked theorem, a reviewed manuscript, a prepared submission, a submitted paper, and an accepted paper. Recommend useful additional review without reporting it as completed or treating advice as policy. Preparation and verification do not authorize contacting specialists, submitting the paper, publishing it, or approving an external gate; those actions follow the user's authorization.
