## Full-size ownership correction

A later 1000×750 Pixel token photograph, with a 12 px travel coefficient, revealed hard depth-band ownership pops that neutral and extreme checks missed. A matched 41-position diagonal sweep measured 9,923 RGB-channel second differences above 32 levels with the original 3 px margin. Margins 12 and 24 raised that proxy count to 11,780 and 14,165. Overlapping ownership transitions at width 0.02 reduced the 3 px case to 60; its maximum second difference fell from 456 to 45. The same sweep and source/depth/texture settings were used. This is a discontinuity proxy on one image, not a quantified aesthetic improvement rate.

The peak-event crops confirmed that an abrupt background-colored flash on the Lifelink token was replaced by a smooth transition. All three margins still had exact neutral source comparisons and opaque extreme outputs. The renderer was corrected and the checker gained a dense-motion probe and peak-event crops so future reviewers can inspect this failure class directly.
